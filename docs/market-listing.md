## Full description

### Increase revenue from every completed order

Help Wix Stores merchants present relevant,
limited-time offers immediately after checkout—when customers are already
engaged with their purchase.

Create campaigns for complementary products, set discount and eligibility
rules, and let the app recommend products using your store catalog. Customers
see a focused offer on the Thank You page and can accept it through Wix
Checkout without changing their original order.

### Key benefits

- **Grow average order value:** Add a second purchase opportunity to the
  post-purchase experience.
- **Make offers more relevant:** Recommend complementary products based on the
  customer’s completed order and your catalog.
- **Launch campaigns quickly:** Configure offer copy, discounts, priorities,
  and order-value rules from the dashboard.
- **Keep checkout familiar:** Accepted offers use Wix Checkout as a separate
  additional purchase.
- **Measure performance:** Track impressions, clicks, accepted offers, and
  attributed revenue in one dashboard.
- **Stay in control:** Use manual campaigns, deterministic rules, AI-assisted,
  or hybrid recommendation modes.

### Features

- Thank You page offer placement for Wix Stores
- Campaign creation, activation, and deletion
- Percentage discount configuration
- Minimum order subtotal targeting
- Manual, rules-based, AI-assisted, and hybrid recommendation modes
- Up to four recommended offers per eligible order
- Configurable headings, subheadings, call-to-action text, colors, and fonts
- Offer dismissal and interaction tracking
- Dashboard analytics for revenue, impressions, click-through rate, and
  conversion rate
- Storefront preview mode for testing the customer experience
- Fallback behavior when AI recommendations are unavailable

### How it works

1. Install the app and open the dashboard.
2. Create a campaign and choose its discount, targeting, and recommendation
   settings.
3. Add the app to the Wix Stores Thank You page.
4. Customers receive relevant offers after a completed order.
5. Accepted offers open a separate Wix Checkout flow, and results appear in
   your dashboard analytics.
